📘 ICT Glossary
Order Block (OB) – Last candle before a significant move. Bullish OB = green, Bearish OB = red.
FVG (Fair Value Gap) – Three-candle gap. Bullish FVG = upside, Bearish FVG = downside.
Liquidity – Sweep of previous highs/lows. Often leads to reversal.
Breaker Block – OB that has been broken and retested.
MSS (Market Structure Shift) – Break of structure, change in direction.
FTA (First Trade Area) – First target after a setup.
SMT (Sellside Liquidity / Buyside Liquidity) – Divergence between price and liquidity.
OTE (Optimal Trade Entry) – The 50-61.8% retracement area.
