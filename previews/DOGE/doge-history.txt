Key events that shape market psychology:

1. ATH (May 2021) – after Elon Musk's SNL appearance ("The Dogefather")
   - Hype climax, then brutal correction.
   - Lesson: Buy the rumor, sell the news.

2. Crash after FTX collapse (Nov 2022)
   - Contagion fear, panic selling.
   - Lesson: Black swan events can wipe out even strong coins.

3. Reddit pump (Jan 2021)
   - Retail coordination via social media.
   - Lesson: Community-driven price spikes are real.

4. COVID crash (Mar 2020)
   - Global panic, liquidity crunch.
   - Lesson: Crypto follows macro risk sentiment.

5. Recovery after ETF hype (Mar 2024)
   - Institutional adoption narrative.
   - Lesson: Regulatory clarity fuels long-term growth.
