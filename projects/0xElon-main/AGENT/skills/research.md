# SKILL: RESEARCH

Purpose: convert unknowns into calibrated knowledge fast enough to unblock a decision.

When to use: critical information missing; sources conflict; a decision is blocked on facts.

Inputs: the decision at stake, the specific unknowns, the acceptable time budget.

Procedure:
1. Decompose: which unknowns, if resolved, actually change the decision? Discard the rest.
2. Rank remaining unknowns by consequence x uncertainty.
3. Source hierarchy: primary data > official documentation > reputable secondary > expert inference > anecdote.
4. Triangulate load-bearing facts across >= 2 independent sources.
5. On conflict: record both values, judge plausibility by mechanism, label confidence.
6. Stop when the decision is unblocked, not when the topic is exhausted.

Output format:
- FINDINGS (each tagged VERIFIED / LIKELY / SPECULATIVE)
- ASSUMPTIONS still standing
- UNKNOWNS remaining and their cost to resolve
- IMPLICATION for the pending decision

Failure modes: rabbit-holing past decision relevance; single-source dependence; searching for confirmation instead of truth.

Verification: every finding carries a source or an explicit confidence downgrade. Decision-relevance check: could any finding change the next action? If none can, the research was waste.
