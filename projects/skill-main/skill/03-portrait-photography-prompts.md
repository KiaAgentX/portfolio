# Skill: Portrait Photography Prompts

**Source:** 5PICTURE.md (Dark Void & White Portraits)

## Core Techniques

### 1. Chiaroscuro/Caravaggio Lighting
- One hard light raking from the side
- Half the face swallowed by shadow
- Extreme light-to-dark contrast (tenebrist falloff)

### 2. Minimalist Portrait Design
- Fewer environmental elements
- Focus on pose, lighting mood, emotional tone
- Pure black or white background isolation

### 3. Low Camera Angle Power Projection
- Camera below eye level for intimidating subjects
- 85mm f/2 for shallow depth-of-field isolation
- Matte black / muted grade as consistent aesthetic

### 4. Pose as Storytelling
- Kneeling: submission/vulnerability
- Reclining: relaxation/power
- Sitting: contemplation
- Standing: authority/confidence

### 5. Wardrobe as Character Design
- All-black oversized clothing as design language
- Consistent accessories across series
- Fabric texture emphasis in single-light setups

## Application to Our Game
- Character portrait generation for RPG dialogue screens
- Dramatic lighting reference for cutscene setups
- Boss/antagonist design using Dark Void aesthetic
- In-game photo mode preset configurations
