⚡ Advanced Order Types

DOGE Vault supports 4 advanced order types beyond simple Buy/Sell:

Limit
Buy below market / Sell above market.
Stop
Buy above market / Sell below market.
OCO
One‑Cancels‑Other. Two orders – one triggers, the other is cancelled.
Auto‑BE
Moves SL to entry when price moves in your favor.
💡 Tip: Use OCO to set a Buy and a Sell at the same time – whichever triggers first cancels the other. This is great for pending breakouts.
